News Headline

Date: DD/MM/YYYY

Black holes (BHs) are one of the most mysterious objects in the universe. Theory showed they form from gravitational collapse of massive stars; observations also showed that galaxies can host supermassive black holes (SMBHs) in their core. How exactly do they evolve and settle in the galaxy nucleus? We are interested in answering such questions by using imaging and spectroscopy data from various facilities (such as HST, JWST, ALMA, ELT) to extract information from the environment around extragalactic BHs.
