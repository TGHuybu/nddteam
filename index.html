Tuan, Tien, and Long Have Been Accepted to Participate in the 2026 IAU International School for Young Astronomers (ISYA) in Vietnam

Date: 04/04/2026

Image: International Centre for Interdisciplinary Science and Education

ISYA is an annual tradition of the International Astronomical Union (IAU). This year's rendition will be held in in Quy Nhon, Vietnam, from 28 September to 16 October 2026. The School will cover a wide range of scientific topics in astronomy and astrophysics, as well as modern analysis tools and techniques, including machine learning and generative artificial intelligence.

Congratulations, Tuan, Tien, and Long! Happy learning and networking!

Hai Ngo Has Been Admitted to the PhD Program at UMich

Date: 21/02/2026

Image: Provided by Hai Ngo

The University of Michigan is a leading public research university in Ann Arbor, Michigan, USA. Its Department of Astronomy is renowned for its education and research in astrophysics, particularly in extragalactic astronomy and black holes. Hai will be joining the UMich PhD program in Fall 2026, where he will continue his research on black holes and galaxies near and far.

Congratulations, Hai! All the absolute best for your PhD journey at UMich and beyond!

Huy Tong Joins Taiwan's ASIAA for a 3-Month Internship

Date: 15/09/2025

Image: Provided by Huy Tong

The International Internship Pilot Program (IIPP) is a yearly internship program, organized and funded by the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC) of Taiwan. It offers international students the opportunity to conduct research at Taiwan's top research institutions for up to 3 months. Since the 2nd of September until the end of November, Huy will be at the Academia Sinica Institute of Astronomy and Astrophysics (ASIAA) for his internship, where he will be working on deep learning methods for astronomical data analysis and forge new connections with the Taiwanese research community.
