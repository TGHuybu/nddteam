Black holes (BHs) are one of the most mysterious objects in the universe. Theory showed they form from gravitational collapse of massive stars, observations also showed that galaxies can host supermassive black holes (SMBHs) in their core. So, how exactly do they evolve and settled in the galaxy nucleus? We are interested in answering such question by using imaging and spectroscopy data from various facilities (such as HST, JWST, ALMA, ELT) to extract information from the environment around extragalactic BHs. We use analysis techniques such as stellar kinematics and gas dynamics to probe the BH's influence and model its properties. We are also exploring with using gravitational lensing as a way to extend the reach to even more distant and ancient galaxies.
