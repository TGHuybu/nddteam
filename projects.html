Intermediate-mass Black Holes in Nearby Dwarf Galaxies

IMBH Project

Identifying and studying intermediate-mass black holes (IMBHs) is crucial for constraining black hole seed formation scenarios and understanding its growth inside galactic cores. Yet, IMBHs remain difficult to detect and measure, due to their small gravitational sphere of influence. In this project, we use state-of-the-art instruments and techniques (namely ...) to uncover the population of IMBHs in nearby dwarf galaxies, and to measure their masses and host galaxy properties.

We are also involved in scientific planning for the upcoming ELT, using mock observations to assert its capabilities for IMBH detection.
